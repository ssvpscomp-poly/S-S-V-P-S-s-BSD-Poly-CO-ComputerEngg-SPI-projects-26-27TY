1\. Project Title: AI Privacy Gateway


2\. Project Description: 



&#x09;AI Privacy Gateway is a privacy-preserving system that acts as an intermediary between users and external AI services. It detects sensitive personal information, analyzes its relevance to the user's query, and masks unnecessary details before forwarding the prompt to the AI service. Its main objective is to protect user privacy while maintaining the usefulness and quality of AI-generated responses.
